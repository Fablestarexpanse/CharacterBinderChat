# Texts, calls and pictures

Characters can text you, call you and send you photos. **All of it is off until you turn it on.**

## Turning it on

1. **Settings → Contact**: switch on **Contact & media**. Here you also set quiet hours, a daily limit, Pause, your
   status (Available, Away, Do not disturb), sounds and notifications.
2. In each character's editor, **Contact**:
   - **May send you photos**
   - **May contact you** — they may text you first after you have been quiet for a while (only while a FableChat tab is
     open). Choose when: when something in the story calls for it, on a routine, or both; how long to wait; and how
     many follow-ups.
   - **May call you** — for what would not wait for a text (making up after trust broke, a long silence) they phone
     instead. **Also call** adds calls now and then, at random times: up to 1–8 a day, at least 1–12 hours apart.

Every message a character sends on their own says why ("reached out because…") with a **Change** link to their
settings.

## Texting

- The **phone icon** in the message box (or **Text** on the character's card) switches the chat to texts: your bubbles
  turn green, like a phone.
- Or just write it into the story: `I text June "you still on air?"` is sent as a real text, only the quoted words,
  and the box switches to texts. Other forms work too: `*I text him* "…"`, `text Elias: on my way`. A line with more
  story after the closing quote is left as you wrote it.
- A reply comes back the way you wrote: text for text, in person for in person.

## Calls

- **Call** from the character's card on the right.
- When a character calls you, their chat in **Recent Chats** rings, with **Answer** and **Decline** right there.
  Nothing pops up over what you are doing.
- Missed calls and unread texts show as a red count on the chat.
- During a call their lines appear as captions while you type back. Spoken calls are coming (see
  [Voice and the narrator](Voice-and-Narrator.md)).

## Photos from a character

A character allowed to send photos may include one in a reply. With **Review before generating** on (Settings →
Contact), you see the prompt first and can change it, take the photo or skip it. Under a photo:

- **Redo** with a changed prompt (the earlier take is kept);
- **Use as portrait** / **Keep as reference** for their image look;
- **Make a clip** — a few seconds of video with sound (see [Images and video](Images-and-Video.md)).

You can also ask: the **camera icon** in the message box → **Ask for a photo**.

## Pictures you send

Use the **picture icon** in the message box, or paste or drop an image. The **picture reader**
([Choosing a chat model](Chat-Models.md)) describes it once, and the character answers knowing what it shows. The
description appears under the picture as "June sees: …", with **Correct** and **Read again**.
